### Question 6
A study tracked 500 office workers for a year: those given standing desks reported 12% less back pain than seated colleagues, while both groups closed the same number of support tickets per week.

Which finding, if true, would best support the claim that standing desks reduce pain without hurting output?

- **A)** Company sales rose during the study year
- **B)** Workers said they liked the standing desks
- **C)** Pain reports fell 12% while ticket counts held steady
- **D)** Standing desks cost more than chairs

What can most reasonably be inferred from the observations?

- **B)** Logging benefits frog populations
- **C)** Every frog in the logged forest died
- **D)** Logging coincided with the loss of frog calls

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

- **B)** Aquarium tanks need stronger locks

### Question 11

- **B)** Mites have been ruled out as a cause
- **C)** The evidence is mixed and the cause is debated
- **D)** Bee populations are not actually declining

#### Question 6
- **Correct Answer: C**
  - **Textual Proof**: Falling pain plus steady tickets is exactly "less pain without hurting output."
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Sales say nothing about pain or tickets.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* Liking desks does not measure pain or output.
  - **D**: *Incorrect.* Cost is irrelevant to the health-productivity claim.

#### Question 7
- **Correct Answer: D**
  - **Textual Proof**: Calls vanished where logging occurred and persisted where it did not — a coincidence of logging with loss.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Movement was never observed; only silence was.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* Loss of calls contradicts any benefit.
  - **C**: *Incorrect.* "Near-total loss of choruses" is not proof every frog died.

#### Question 8
- **Correct Answer: C**
  - **Textual Proof**: Escapes, jar-opening, keeper recognition, and latch-defeating all illustrate intelligent problem-solving.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* No keeper was harmed; mischief is not danger.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* Locks are a detail, not the main idea.
  - **D**: *Incorrect.* Keeper kindness is background, not the point.

#### Question 9
- **Correct Answer: D**
  - **Textual Proof**: An 18% drop in downtown car entries the launch year directly evidences fewer car trips.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Bike color is irrelevant.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* One rider proves nothing about trips.
  - **C**: *Incorrect.* "Many unrelated reasons" weakens rather than supports the claim.

#### Question 10
- **Correct Answer: B**
  - **Textual Proof**: Majorities first-time (60%) and under forty (80%) compose a young, new audience.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Directly contradicted by 60% first-timers.
  - **C**: *Incorrect.* Nothing about older attitudes was measured.
  - **D**: *Incorrect.* Price was never surveyed.

#### Question 11
- **Correct Answer: C**
  - **Textual Proof**: Competing findings (pesticides vs. mites) with recovery under mite treatment leave the cause genuinely debated.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* "Sole cause" overstates contested evidence.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* Mites predict collapse best; they are not ruled out.
  - **D**: *Incorrect.* Decline itself is undisputed in all four studies.

#### Question 12
- **Correct Answer: D**
  - **Textual Proof**: "Quietly, without pay," six years of work, herons returning — the framing admires her.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Nothing scornful appears.
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* A full admiring portrait is not indifference.
  - **C**: *Incorrect.* No doubt about her work is raised.
