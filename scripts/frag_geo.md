### Question 6
- **Correct Answer: D ($12\pi$)**
- **Method 1 (Sector Formula)**:
  $$120^\circ = \frac{2\pi}{3} \text{ radians}, \qquad A = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \theta = \frac{1}{2}(36)\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 12\pi$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($4\pi$)**: Mixed radius with diameter in the setup.
  - **B ($6\pi$)**: Halved the angle ($\pi/3$ instead of $2\pi/3$).
  - **C ($24\pi$)**: Forgot the $\frac{1}{2}$ factor.

### Question 7
- **Correct Answer: B ($45$)**
- **Method 1 (Scale the Perimeter)**:
  Perimeters scale with side lengths: $18 \cdot \frac{5}{2} = 45$.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($36$)**: Doubled instead of scaling by $5/2$.
  - **C ($7.2$)**: Inverted the ratio ($18 \cdot 2/5$).
  - **D ($90$)**: Scaled area-style (squared thinking) or doubled twice.

### Question 8

- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($5$)**: Reported the short leg instead of the long leg.
  - **B ($5\sqrt{2}$)**: Used the 45-45-90 pattern.
  - **C ($10\sqrt{3}$)**: Scaled the long leg from the hypotenuse incorrectly.

### Question 9
- **Correct Answer: A ($36\pi$)**
- **Method 1 (Cone Volume)**:
  $$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (9)(12) = 36\pi$$

  - **C ($12\pi$)**: Used $r$ instead of $r^2$.
  - **D ($18\pi$)**: Halved the correct value.

### Question 10
- **Correct Answer: C ($\frac{5\pi}{6}$)**
- **Method 1 (Conversion Factor)**:
  $$150^\circ \cdot \frac{\pi}{180^\circ} = \frac{150\pi}{180} = \frac{5\pi}{6}$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($\frac{5\pi}{3}$)**: Doubled the correct value.
  - **B ($\frac{6\pi}{5}$)**: Inverted the conversion fraction.
  - **D ($\frac{2\pi}{5}$)**: Mis-simplified $150/180$.

### Question 11
- **Correct Answer: D ($30$)**
- **Method 1 (Find the Missing Leg First)**:
  Other leg $= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ (a 5-12-13 triangle).
  $$A = \frac{1}{2}(5)(12) = 30$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($32.5$)**: Used the hypotenuse as a leg: $13 \cdot 5 / 2$.
  - **B ($60$)**: Forgot to halve: $5 \cdot 12$.
  - **C ($39$)**: Mixed hypotenuse into the area product.

---

### Question 12
- **Correct Answer: 36**
- **Method 1 (Sphere Volume)**:
  $$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (27) = 36\pi \implies \frac{V}{\pi} = 36$$
