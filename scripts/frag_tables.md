#### Question 5

  - **A**: *Incorrect.* Every year exceeds the last.

#### Question 6
- **Correct Answer: A (40%)**
  - **Textual Proof**: Bicycle (25%) plus walking (15%) totals 40%.
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **B**: *Incorrect.* Bicycle share alone, omitting walkers.
  - **C**: *Incorrect.* Walking share alone, omitting cyclists.
  - **D**: *Incorrect.* Adds the car share instead.

#### Question 7
