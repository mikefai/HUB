### Question 6
- **Correct Answer: D ($25\%$)**
- **Method 1 (Change over Original)**:
  $$\frac{100 - 80}{80} = \frac{20}{80} = 0.25 = 25\%$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($20\%$)**: Reported the dollar difference as a percent.
  - **B ($80\%$)**: Divided old price by new price ($80/100$).
  - **C ($125\%$)**: Reported new/old ($100/80$) instead of the increase.

### Question 7
- **Correct Answer: D ($10$)**
- **Method 1 (Proportion)**:
  $$\frac{3}{2} = \frac{15}{s} \implies 3s = 30 \implies s = 10$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($6$)**: Scaled by the wrong factor.
  - **B ($7.5$)**: Halved the flour instead of applying the $3:2$ ratio.
  - **C ($22.5$)**: Inverted the ratio ($15 \cdot 3/2$).

### Question 8
- **Correct Answer: B ($\frac{1}{4}$)**
- **Method 1 (Favorable over Total)**:
  Total $= 4 + 5 + 11 = 20$. $$P(\text{blue}) = \frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4}$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($\frac{5}{11}$)**: Compared blue to green only.
  - **C ($\frac{1}{3}$)**: Compared blue to non-green ($5/15$).
  - **D ($\frac{11}{20}$)**: Gave the probability of green instead.

### Question 9
- **Correct Answer: C ($7$)**
- **Method 1 (Weighted Mean)**:
  $$\frac{6(5) + 4(3) + 14(2)}{10} = \frac{30 + 12 + 28}{10} = \frac{70}{10} = 7$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($8$)**: Averaged the three values ignoring weights: $(6 + 4 + 14)/3$.
  - **B ($6$)**: Reported the median instead of the mean.
  - **D ($10$)**: Reported the range ($14 - 4$).

### Question 10
- **Correct Answer: A ($95$)**
- **Method 1 (Substitute into the Model)**:
  $$y = 5(7) + 60 = 35 + 60 = 95$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **B ($35$)**: Used the slope term only, dropping the intercept.
  - **C ($60$)**: Reported the intercept (zero hours of study).
  - **D ($420$)**: Multiplied intercept by hours ($60 \cdot 7$).

### Question 11
- **Correct Answer: D ($88$)**
- **Method 1 (Convert Units)**:
  $$60\ \frac{\text{mi}}{\text{h}} \cdot \frac{5{,}280\text{ ft}}{1\text{ mi}} \cdot \frac{1\text{ h}}{3{,}600\text{ s}} = \frac{316{,}800}{3{,}600} = 88\text{ ft/s}$$
- **Distractor Analysis**:
  - **A ($5{,}280$)**: Converted miles but forgot time entirely.
  - **B ($60$)**: Kept the number, ignoring units.
  - **C ($44$)**: Halved the correct value.

---

### Question 12
- **Correct Answer: 27**
- **Method 1 (Parts of a Whole)**:
  $$3x + 4x = 63 \implies 7x = 63 \implies x = 9$$
  Smaller number $= 3(9) = 27$.
- **Verification**: $27 + 36 = 63$ and $27/36 = 3/4$. ✓
