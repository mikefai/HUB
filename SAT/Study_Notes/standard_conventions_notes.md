## Core Rules

- **Boundaries**: two independent clauses join with `;` / `.` / `, + and-but-or`. A comma alone is a splice; a semicolon after a fragment fails.
- **Dependent clauses** (*because, although, while*) attach with a comma â€” never a semicolon or period alone.
- **Subject-verb**: find the true subject by deleting prepositional phrases ("The list of ingredients *is*â€¦").
- **Neither/nor**: the verb and pronoun agree with the nearer subject ("â€¦nor the employees â€¦ *their*").
- **Tense**: timelines rule â€” "by the time X happened" triggers past perfect (*had awaited*); time clauses use present for future meaning.
- **Nonrestrictive clauses** (extra info about a unique noun): paired commas + *which*. Restrictive (identifying): no commas.
- **Parallelism**: match forms (*hiking, swimming, biking*); *not only* pairs with *but also*.
- **Possessives**: singular *judge's*, plural *judges'*. Pronouns: singular antecedent â†’ singular pronoun.

## Common Traps

- Agreeing with the nearest noun instead of the subject.
- *Which* without commas (implies multiples); *that* after a comma.
- Choosing a true fact in the wrong tense.
